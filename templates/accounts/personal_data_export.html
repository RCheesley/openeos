What the archive contains

  • Your account: username, name, email, sign-in dates, your profile, whether two-factor sign-in is on, and your notification preferences.
  • Your memberships: each organisation you belong to, your role and your teams.
  • Records that name you: every {{ terms.rock }}, {{ terms.todo }}, {{ terms.issue }}, metric, meeting entry, note and edit where you are the owner, creator, author or participant, with its team, organisation and dates.
  • Your activity: sign-ins and other actions recorded under your account.
  • Your avatar, and a README that explains the files.

Your password, two-factor secrets, backup codes and sign-in sessions are never included, and other people's names are left out.
